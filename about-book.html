Newton's 3rd Law

by Bibek Patra Science

Applying the principles of action and reaction to real life — a practical guide to understanding cause and effect in everyday situations.

Read Full Description

Newton's 3rd Law — for every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction. But what does this mean beyond physics? Bibek Patra brilliantly translates this universal law into the language of everyday life, showing how every choice, every word, and every action creates a ripple effect.

This book is a practical guide to understanding the invisible forces that shape our relationships, careers, and personal growth. Through real-life examples and thought-provoking insights, readers learn how to harness the power of cause and effect to create meaningful change.

Whether you're navigating a difficult situation or striving for a breakthrough, Newton's 3rd Law offers a fresh perspective on how to take intentional action and navigate the reactions that follow with grace and clarity.

Akele Nahi Ho

by Bibek Patra Poetry

Read Full Description
