About Me

Founder · Developer · Author · Content Creator · 3D Designer

Building the Future, One Innovation at a Time

I'm Bibek Patra — a founder, developer, author, and creative technologist from Mayurbhanj, Odisha. I build digital products that merge design, intelligence, and purpose. From AI assistants to social platforms, publishing tools to 3D visualization — my work is driven by a simple belief: technology should empower people.

I don't just write code. I write books. I build websites. I create content that educates and inspires. I architect experiences. And I'm constantly pushing the boundaries of what's possible — blending art, engineering, and entrepreneurship into everything I create.
